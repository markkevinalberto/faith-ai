Why local beats cloud here

Health data is the last thing that should need a signal

Private by construction

Records, voice and photos of prescriptions never leave the phone. The database is encrypted with SQLCipher and the key lives in the Android Keystore. No analytics, no crash reporting, no account.

Works where care happens

Clinics with no Wi-Fi, provincial roads, brownouts, a prepaid plan that ran out. The assistant does not care. Models download once, then the internet is optional.

Free to run, forever

No per-question cost and no subscription. Dose reminders are local notifications that survive a reboot. A phone with 3 GB of RAM can run the smaller models.

Grounded in your own records

Every number in an answer is computed by tested code from what you recorded. The language model only rephrases, and an output guard rejects anything it invents.

What the AI does, on the phone

Four things that keep working

Each one is optional and each one has a non-AI fallback, so the app is useful on any phone and even better with the models installed.

Ask FAITH

Ask about readings, medicines, lab results or appointments in plain words. The answer always has three labelled parts, so you can see what was computed, what came from the library, and what the model wrote.

  • “I missed my metformin, should I take two?” is refused before the model is called.
  • Statistics, trends and unit conversions (7.2 mmol/L is 130 mg/dL) come from deterministic code.
  • Targets show who set them. Reference ranges are labelled “not personalised”.

Scan labels and lab reports

Photograph a pharmacy label or a results table. Text is recognised on the phone, a parser copies what is printed, and the language model fills in only the fields the parser missed.

  • Every value the model proposes must appear in the scanned text. Anything else is dropped and you are told.
  • Reminder times are suggested from the printed frequency, for example “twice daily” becomes 08:00 and 20:00, and labelled as a starting point.
  • Nothing is saved until you check it. The photo is deleted after scanning unless you keep it with a lab record.

Voice questions and voice logging

Tap the microphone, speak, tap again. Whisper turns it into text on the phone. In the reading form, “sugar 145 fasting” or “weight 72.5 kilos” fills the fields for you to confirm.

  • Audio is held in memory only, never written to disk, never uploaded.
  • Recording lasts at most 20 seconds and only between your two taps.
  • A spoken reading is a draft. You still press Save.

Search by meaning

A small embedding model finds records and library articles that match what you mean, not just the words you used. “Did I ever feel dizzy?” finds the note that says “felt shaky after a long walk”. Keyword matches are always kept too.

  • Embeddings live in memory and are cleared when you switch profiles.
  • The model is 25 MB and loads in a moment when Ask opens.

Built to be safe

A companion, not a clinician

FAITH organises and explains. It does not diagnose, prescribe or change treatment, and the code enforces that, not just the wording.

A dose is never marked taken by itself

Only you can tap “Taken”. The database itself rejects a system-recorded dose. An overdue dose becomes “not confirmed”, never “missed, take it now”.

No dose advice, ever

Questions about doubling, skipping, stopping or making up doses are refused before any model runs. Generated text that contains dose instructions is thrown away.

Numbers are computed, not generated

Averages, ranges, trends, conversions and refill estimates come from unit-tested code. The guard rejects any number in an answer that is not in the facts it was given.

Every target shows its source

A clinician-entered target is labelled with who set it and when. Otherwise you see a general reference range with its citation, and nothing is ever called “safe”.

Escalation comes first

Emergency phrases and dangerous readings short-circuit everything else into a card with steps and a Call button. Thresholds follow ADA, AHA, ISH and WHO guidance and are marked draft, pending clinical review.

  • Glucose below 54 mg/dL (3.0 mmol/L), or 300 mg/dL (16.7 mmol/L) and above: urgent
  • Blood pressure 180 systolic or 120 diastolic and above: urgent, emergency if there are symptoms
  • Oxygen saturation below 90%, temperature below 35 °C: urgent

How an answer is built

The model is the last step, not the first

  1. Safety routerEmergency phrases and dose-change requests are caught here, with no model involved.
  2. Your recordsReadings, medicines, labs and appointments for the active profile only.
  3. CalculationsAverages, ranges, trends and conversions from tested code.
  4. LibraryCurated articles with sources and review dates, found by keyword and by meaning.
  5. On-device modelRephrases the facts into plain language. It cannot add facts.
  6. Output guardChecks every number and refuses dose, diagnosis or “you’re fine” language.

Try it

Install, add the sample person, switch off the signal

The APK installs without a store. A fictional sample profile with 90 days of data lets you explore every screen before entering anything real.

  1. Download the APK on your Android phoneAndroid 8 or newer, 64-bit. If asked, allow installs from your browser.
  2. Open FAITH and add the sample data profileEverything is labelled as sample data and no reminders are scheduled for it.
  3. Install a model in Settings → On-device AIWi-Fi recommended. Start with the 25 MB search model, then Whisper, then Qwen if the phone has 4 GB of RAM or more.
  4. Turn on airplane mode and ask awayThe Ask screen badge turns green when the phone is offline and the AI is still running.
